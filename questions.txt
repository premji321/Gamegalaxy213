SUBJECT: Civics
CHAPTER: Federalism
LEVEL: Easy
Q: What is federalism?
A: A system in which power is divided between different levels of government
B: A system in which only the central government has power
C: A system without elections
D: A system in which judges make all decisions
ANSWER: A
EXPLANATION: Federalism is a system of government in which power is divided between a central authority and various constituent units of the country.

Q: Which of the following is a key feature of federalism?
A: One level of government controls all powers
B: Different levels of government have their own jurisdiction
C: State governments are abolished
D: The Constitution has no importance
ANSWER: B
EXPLANATION: In a federal system, different levels of government have their own constitutionally defined powers and responsibilities.
